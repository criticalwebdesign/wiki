0. "Designers […] hack the present to create the conditions of the future."<br />—Anne Balsamo, *Designing Culture: The Technological Imagination at Work* (2011)
1. "Cyberspace. A consensual hallucination experienced daily by billions of legitimate operators, in every nation, by children being taught mathematical concepts…A graphic representation of data abstracted from the banks of every computer in the human system. Unthinkable complexity. Lines of light ranged in the nonspace of the mind, clusters and constellation of data. Like city lights, receding…"<br />—William Gibson, *Neuromancer* (1984)
2. "Codes typically serve three main purposes. They are used for communication, clarification, or obfuscation."<br />—Casey Reas, Chandler McWilliams, and LUST, *Form+Code in Design, Art, and Architecture* (2010)
3. "Most North Americans now live designer lives—sleep, eat, sit in a car, work, shop, watch TV, sleep again. I doubt there's more than a handful of free, spontaneous minutes anywhere in that cycle."<br />—Kalle Lasn, *Culture Jam* (2000)
4. "There is a profound and enduring beauty in simplicity; in clarity, in efficiency. True simplicity is derived from so much more than just the absence of clutter and ornamentation. It’s about bringing order to complexity."<br />—Jony Ive "Forward" for *As Little Design as Possible* by Dieter Rams (2024)
5. "Don't make me think."<br />—Steve Krug, *Don't Make Me Think* (2000)
6. “Collage...[is] a kind of catch-all metaphor for our overly technologized lives.” <br />—Jessica Helfand, Screen: Essays on Graphic Design, New Media, and Visual Culture (2001, Princeton Architectural Press)
7. "For most of us, design is invisible. Until it fails."<br />—Bruce Mau, *Massive Change* (2004)
8. "...remixed media may quote sounds over images, or video over text, or text over sounds. The quotes thus get mixed together. The mix produces the new creative work—the 'remix.'"<br />—Lawrence Lessig, Remix (2008)
9. "Surveillance capitalism unilaterally claims human experience as free raw material for translation into behavioral data."<br />―Shoshana Zuboff, The Age of Surveillance Capitalism (2019)
10. "[As designers] our job is to advocate for the people who aren't in the room. [...] We need to understand we have a greater responsibility to society than to the people who sign the checks."<br />—Mike Monteiro, Ruined by Design (2019)
11. "Before enlightenment, chop wood, carry water. After enlightenment, chop wood, carry water."<br />—Zen Kōan
