As you complete the modules in this chapter, you will use design iteration to create two layouts.
One will use a grid to align text along rows and columns. The second will use the same
content in an experimental composition. For the text in your layout, you could sample a
different line from a manifesto that you liked in chapter 2 or create your own using the
haiku exercise below while taking a break from the internet.

- Preview the [live page](https://criticalwebdesign.github.io/book/06-off-the-grid/6-3/) and [work files](https://github.com/criticalwebdesign/book/tree/main/06-off-the-grid)
